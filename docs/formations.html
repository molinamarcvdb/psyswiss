How to read this + what “MAS/DAS” means for jobs

MAS (Master of Advanced Studies) = the long postgraduate diploma (2–6 yrs alongside work). Accredited psychotherapy MAS tracks are what lead to the federal title. DAS = shorter diploma — deeper than a CAS but not enough alone for the title. CAS = short modules, add-ons only.

Does orientation change job prospects? From our 133-ad scrape: barely at entry level. Employers filter on en formation + French C1 + experience. At the margins: CBT is most cited (addictions, hospitals); systemic is valued in family/child/violence settings (MalleyPrairie asks for it); psychodynamic skews to private practice. Example: Mediversité Nyon requires MAS enrollment, any orientation; Clinique Belmont prefers CBT or any MAS/DAS in progress; La Consultation welcomes systemic, CBT, psychodynamic, ACT equally.

Deadlines pass fast and cohorts fill 1–2 years ahead (UNIL systemic: next cohort Nov 2027, sign-up early 2027; PSP Basel adult track: Jan 2029). Shortlist 2–3 tracks now and join their alert lists even before recognition lands.
