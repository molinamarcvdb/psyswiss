0 · Can she work before the recognition or the training? — Yes

Key answer

Working before the PsyKo recognition and before the psychotherapy training is not only allowed — it is the normal path. What is blocked until recognition is narrower than most people fear:

✖ Blocked for now: using the protected title « Psychologue / Psychologin » professionally (needs a recognised Master's — a bachelor's alone gives no title at all); entering an accredited psychotherapy programme (institutes may not set their own equivalence — PsyKo recognition of the German MSc is the mandatory ticket); and working en propre responsabilité (independent practice — that comes much later, with the federal therapy title + cantonal licence).

✔ Allowed now: Swiss law leaves employed, supervised work to the cantons. So she can work today as long as it is under supervision, without the protected titles and without independent practice. The right job labels at this stage:

  • assistante psychologue / stagiaire psychologue / collaboratrice
  • intervenante psychosociale, auxiliaire, remplaçante
  • research / quality / internship posts (e.g. the HUG DynamO internship in our scrape)

This is exactly why the live ads are written the way they are: La Consultation hires « en formation postgrade » and Clinique Belmont accepts « MAS/DAS en cours » — employers expect candidates whose recognition is in progress. The credential to quote in every email: « MSc Psychologie (Allemagne), reconnaissance PsyKo en cours, dossier n° … »

Two warnings so the years really prepare her:

  • Pre-training work does not shorten the training. The 2 years of clinical practice, 500h of own therapy work, supervision, etc. must be done during the accredited programme at recognised sites (narrow exceptions only). So pick bridge jobs for skills, French and network — and at every interview ask: « Êtes-vous reconnu comme lieu de pratique clinique par les filières accréditées ? »
  • Don't oversell the title. On CVs and signatures write the degree (“MSc Psychology, Germany”), never “psychologue” — let the employer assign the supervised job title.

Basis: BAG/PsyKo rules (PsyG; employed supervised work is cantonal competence; protected titles need recognised Master's). Information only — the canton and PsyKo decide each case.
Practical order: PsyKo receipt → French C1 evidence → spontaneous supervised/assistant applications → accredited training application once recognition lands.

1 · How people generally get in — the pattern

  1. Recognition first, training second. Institutes hire on the condition “Master en psychologie (ou titre équivalent reconnu par la Commission des psychologues)” — a real quote from La Consultation's live ad (posted 6 Sept 2026, in our scrape). The PsyKo dossier number alone already makes a spontaneous application credible. Mention it in every email.
  2. French C1 is the gatekeeper. Both live clinical ads require it explicitly (“certificat C1 pour non-francophones” / “parfaite maîtrise du français”). With a Spanish + German background, certified French is the single highest-ROI move — name the level honestly in each email.
  3. Target “en formation” roles. La Consultation explicitly hires psychologues en formation postgrade; Clinique Belmont accepts candidates en cours de MAS/DAS. These are the realistic entry points — not fully licensed posts.
  4. Make the bridge job count. The accredited training requires ≥2 years of clinical practice (≥1 year in a psychotherapeutic-psychiatric setting) during the training, at recognised practice sites. At every interview ask: « L’institution est-elle reconnue comme lieu de pratique clinique par les filières de formation accréditées ? » — otherwise the years may not count later.
  5. Hospitals hire via portals, not email. HUG accepts exclusively electronic applications through SmartRecruiters. Don't email a CV into the void — apply to the right category and call it out.

3 · Attachments to join to every application

  • CV (French, 1–2 pages) + diplomas (Spanish bachelor, German MSc) + transcript showing clinical-psychology credits.
  • PsyKo acknowledgement of receipt / dossier number — the single strongest signal while recognition is pending.
  • French certificate (or honest level + exam date if C1 is in progress).
  • Extrait de casier judiciaire — both clinical ads require a clean record; join it pre-emptively.
  • One line on the targeted postgraduate track (approach + institute shortlist) — shows the training plan is real.
