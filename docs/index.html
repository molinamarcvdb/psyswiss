1 · The path in 6 steps

1 Master recognised by PsyKo. Foreign MSc must be recognised as equivalent (PsyG Art. 3). Bachelor alone gives no title.
2 Clinical credits. Psychotherapy track needs sufficient clinical psychology / psychopathology — institutes check case by case.
3 Accredited training. 2–6 yr BAG-accredited programme (theory, 2 yrs practice, 500h own work, supervision) — or foreign therapy title recognised.
4 Federal title + PsyReg. « Eidg. anerkannte/r Psychotherapeut/in », public register entry (CHF 250).
5 Cantonal licence. Needed for work in own professional responsibility, from the canton of practice.
6 Billing. Since 07/2022 the prescription model allows billing basic insurance on a doctor's prescription.

Sources: BAG recognition · PsyG FAQ · PsyReg · Accredited programmes

2 · Can she work before recognition? — Yes

Blocked: the protected title « Psychologue », entering an accredited programme (only PsyKo recognition opens it), independent practice.

Allowed now: employed, supervised work — assistante psychologue, stagiaire, intervenante psychosociale, auxiliaire, remplaçante. Employers expect in-progress recognition: quote « MSc Psychologie (Allemagne), reconnaissance PsyKo en cours, dossier n° … ».

Catches: pre-training years don't shorten the training (practice must be during it, at recognised sites — ask « lieu de pratique reconnu ? »). Write the degree on the CV, never “psychologue”. French C1 is the gatekeeper for clinical posts.
